# Product brief and build plan

Written 10 Oct 2026. The fuller version, with costs and sources, is the doc
"Reel tool for production companies: product brief and build plan" in Claude.

## What it is

A hosted tool for production companies and reps that builds the right reel for a
brief and learns from what happens after it is sent. The software is not the edge.
The edge is the method for reading spots and briefs, plus the record of outcomes it
is tuned on. Fox Den is the first user and the test bed.

## The loop

1. Library: every director's spots in one hosted place.
2. Analysis: an AI model watches and listens to each spot and records what is in it.
3. Brief in: a rep adds the brief, the agency and the client.
4. Reel out: a proposed reel, with the reason for every pick.
5. Outcome back: opened, watched, shortlisted, awarded.
6. Learning: outcomes change the next recommendation.

Steps 1 to 4 are a search tool. Steps 5 and 6 are the product.

## The method

Picking a reel is four separate judgments, each tested on its own:

1. Reading the spot: what does a creative director respond to in this commercial?
2. Reading the brief: what is the agency asking for underneath the words?
3. Scoring the fit: how well does this spot answer this brief?
4. Building the reel: which spots, in what order, with how much range?

Rules: winning is defined as shortlisted. Every pick comes with its reason. The
system states how strong its evidence is; it supports judgment and does not predict.

## Stages

1. Fox Den only. Sign-in for the team, the library, upload with analysis of picture
   and sound, the private-notes form, brief in and reel out, reels that play and
   share by link, results logged by hand.
2. Close the loop. Viewing data arrives on its own; agency profiles; reports.
3. Outside customers. Separate company accounts, billing, onboarding, legal terms,
   and an outside security review before a second company's data goes in.

## Build order inside stage 1

1. Accounts and the empty library.
2. Upload one spot and play it back.
3. AI analysis of one spot, shown as an editable record.
4. Bulk upload of a director's folder.
5. Brief in, ranked spots out.
6. Reel builder: reorder, swap, share link.
7. Submission and result log.
